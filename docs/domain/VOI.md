# VOI extractor

Scope: VOI candidates from a segmentation set, sides, previews, the `voi.extract` task and its outputs.
Read when: working on `plugins/voi/`, `backend/app/voi/` or `frontend/src/plugins/voi/`.
Depends: ADR-0050, TASKS.md (protocol, outputs), PLUGINS.md, SOURCES.md (SRC-12).

## Requirements

| ID | Requirement | Pri |
|---|---|---|
| VOI-01 | `voi.extract` runs from the common task tab (UI-20): Selection (all scans with a mask, the Explorer filter, an item list, or the viewer's current scan or case frozen into the list), the segmentation set, the plugin's own Settings, the preflight with the **review** step, Run and the runs. There is no other surface: the Tasks view, menu and palette open this tab. | M |
| VOI-02 | A **region** preset sets the labels, laterality, minimum component volume, margin and minimum size: kidney and lung give one VOI per side (`L`, `R`); liver, pancreas, stomach, heart and custom give one VOI (`V1`) around all kept parts. A region needs one of its **organ** labels in the project (kidney: `kidney`, `kidney_left`, `kidney_right`; lung: `lung`, `lung_left`, `lung_right`; pancreas: `pancreas` or its head, body and tail), so another organ's `tumor` never makes a liver VOI. Settings show the region's labels found in the chosen segmentation set; **Customize…** lists the set's active labels (its mapping minus removed labels) as checkboxes and the pick replaces the region's labels (`labels`; then no organ check); with no label nothing is found or run. The **Box** is Recommended (the region's margin and minimum size, shown in mm), Tight (margin 0, no minimum: the label box) or Custom (editable margin per side and x, y, z minimum); the effective size is always shown. The API accepts no labels (the region's names; ones the project lacks are a note) and a custom region needs labels. | M |
| VOI-03 | Components smaller than the region's minimum volume or 1 % of the largest are dropped, except fragments that hold a protected label (tumour, nodule), which join the nearest kept box (`protected_component_merged`). | M |
| VOI-04 | Sides come from patient (world) coordinates on the canonical RAS grid of the **image** (ADR-0050): a mask of the same shape is read voxel for voxel on the image's geometry, whatever its own header says (`mask_header_differs` when they disagree); a different shape is refused. Two parts are ordered right to left; one part is compared with the body midline (median of the body's soft tissue, table cut off; `midline_from_grid` when no body is found); within 15 mm of it the side is `side_ambiguous`; a blob crossing the midline with both halves away from it is split (`split_at_midline`); two parts on one side are `both_on_one_side_of_midline`; an oblique grid is `oblique_grid`; labels touching the scan edge are `at_image_edge`. When the mask has a liver or spleen, a liver on the patient's left of the spleen (or of the midline) by more than 30 mm means the header mirrors left and right: the sides are swapped and flagged `sides_from_anatomy`. When label names state a side (`kidney_left`), a VOI whose labels mostly name the other side is `label_side_mismatch`. | M |
| VOI-05 | The crop box is the label box plus the margin, grown to the minimum size; a side blocked by the image edge gives its share to the other side, and the box never leaves the image. | M |
| VOI-06 | Each VOI shows its preview (the crop's middle axial slice with the label bounding box in yellow and the crop box in blue, R/L marked, anterior up, patient right on the image left; no contours), the label box and crop sizes in mm (right–left × anterior–posterior × superior–inferior), the labelled volume in mL (with each label's share), where its side came from (label names, position in the patient, or liver and spleen) and its warnings. VOIs that need review (an uncertain side or any warning) are shown in full; the others as compact rows unless expanded. The preview never changes the main viewer. | M |
| VOI-12 | Every lateral VOI shows how sure its side is: **Verified** (its side-named labels agree, or liver/spleen confirm the header and the VOI lies at least 15 mm on its side of the midline with no doubt), **Probable** (coordinates only, nothing against), **Uncertain** (`side_ambiguous`, `both_on_one_side_of_midline`, `label_side_mismatch` or `split_at_midline`; or `midline_from_grid`, `oblique_grid`, `mask_header_differs` without confirming labels). **Swap L/R** (ringed when a side is uncertain) swaps the scan's L and R at once, **Undo swap** reverts it; nothing is written until Run. A swapped side reads **Corrected**, and the catalog stores `side_confidence` (`reviewer` after a swap). On 300 PanTS scans (2026-11-09) every Verified kidney side matched its labels (521/521) and every wrong side was Uncertain. | M |
| VOI-07 | The review step under the preflight works from one scan to thousands. One scan's VOIs are found at once; a batch needs **Find VOIs** (the first 50 scans, then each page as it is opened), and **Check the other N scans** finds the rest in the background (4 at a time, progress, cancel). Changing the region, labels or segmentation set asks for it again; margin and size changes reframe instantly and keep the counts. The list is 50 scans per page with counts over what has been found (VOIs, included, left out, uncertain sides, warnings, failed scans, scans checked of N), a filter All · Needs review, **Next needs review** and Expand all. The reviewer swaps a scan's sides and leaves single VOIs out; the decisions live in the settings as `overrides` keyed by scan and VOI, survive box changes and page changes, and decisions on scans or VOIs no longer there are listed with **Drop them** (never dropped silently). Run never waits for the review: scans not checked are extracted with the detected sides, which the review says, and anything uncertain is listed in the run's results (VOI-10). Run starts `voi.extract` for the ready scans, stamped with the reviewer (TSK-10). | M |
| VOI-08 | `voi.extract` writes, per kept VOI, `{case}/{item}_{side}_image.nii.gz` and `…_mask.nii.gz` in RAS into the run's `output_dir` (derived root, R1), optionally resampled to isotropic voxels (`resample_mm`, 0 = native; image cubic, mask nearest), the mask with every label or binary (`keep_labels`), and appends one row per VOI to `voi_catalog.jsonl` (identity, side, swap, region, labels and their values in the mask crop, paths, shape, spacing, source crop, sizes, volume, warnings). Items run on a thread pool (`workers`); a scan whose VOIs are all excluded is `skipped`. | M |
| VOI-09 | The slow step (reading the mask, components, midline) runs in a job worker and is cached per image, mask, labels and region under `cache/voi/`; margin and size changes are recomputed from that cache without reading volumes. A run's catalog is read once and cached until the file changes, so paging and filtering it does not parse it again. | S |
| VOI-13 | `voi.extract` crops only **complete** scans (`requires.scope`): a scan already imported as a VOI crop (scope `voi`) is never cropped again, and a scan that already has VOI items in the project is skipped (`requires.without_vois`) unless **Skip scans that already have VOIs** is turned off in Settings (`skip_existing`, on by default; the scan is then extracted again into the run's folder and the project's VOIs are not replaced). Both show in the preflight as `voi item, needs complete` and `already has VOIs` and update as the option changes, outside the ready list and the review, and are skipped, never failed. Identity is per scan, scope and side, so the same VOI as npy and NIfTI is one item, not two. Outputs are NIfTI only (VOI-08). | M |
| VOI-11 | The task's run rows (task tab and Tasks view) offer **Show results** on a finished run. | M |
| VOI-10 | **Show results** opens the run in the same tab as a read-only snapshot that stays usable at any size: status, age, reviewer and counts, the settings as they were (region, labels, box, output voxel), the folder with a Copy action, and a summary of the whole run (cases, scans, VOIs) whose counts are also the filters: All · Verified · Probable · Uncertain · Corrected · Needs review, plus a search over case and scan ids and a warning filter. The list shows at most 50 rows per page, **by scan** (one row per scan with its side and confidence badges and a warnings count) or **by case** (scans, VOIs and how many need review; a case opens its scans with a breadcrumb back to all cases); a run with five or more scans per case opens by case, otherwise by scan. A scan row opens to its VOIs: a preview of the crop itself (the middle axial slice of its label box, with that box drawn; R/L marked), side (Corrected when the reviewer swapped it), shape, voxel size, size in mm, labelled volume, warnings and file names; previews load only when a row is opened. A run that is still going says so and its VOIs appear when it ends. **Back to Extract VOIs** returns to the form. | M |

## Outputs

`voi_catalog.jsonl` paths are relative to the run's `output_dir`; `orientation` is always `RAS` and `format` `nifti`. The catalog is not imported automatically; import it through IMP-10 when the crops should become project items.
